# Growing a small vegetable bed: first notes

A small bed is a gentle way to learn how plants behave through the season. Good starting references are collected at [garden planning notes](https://example.org/garden/), where the basics of sun, soil and watering are explained.

## Choosing the place

Most vegetables like at least six hours of sun. Watch your yard for a day and note which corner stays bright the longest.

## Soil and watering

Loose, crumbly soil with a little compost works for beginners. Water in the morning, deeper but less often, so the roots go down.

## What to grow first

Lettuce, radishes and herbs are forgiving and quick. Add beans or tomatoes once you are comfortable with the routine.
